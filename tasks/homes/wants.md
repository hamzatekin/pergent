# What I want from a rented home in Nottingham

I rent on my own. My family (3 or 4 people) come to visit and need somewhere to sleep, so a second
bedroom matters. A one-bedroom place can still be right if it is clearly better in every other way and
has a separate living room big enough for a sofa bed. Studios are out.

## Hard limits

- Rent at most £850 a month when bills are not included, or £1,000 when they are.
- EPC rating C or better (A, B or C). D or worse is a no.
- 1 or 2 bedrooms (3 is fine if it is within the rent).

## Getting around

- I have no car today. Private parking, such as a driveway, is still a plus.
- I work at NG8 6AT, next to the Phoenix Park tram stop.
- I go into the city centre often, to the Motorpoint Arena for Nottingham Panthers games and to the
  City Ground for Nottingham Forest games, most weeks.
- I like walking: today I live at NG5 2DU in Sherwood and walk 25 minutes to The Forest tram stop.
  That will not work in a cold, wet winter, so a short bus or tram ride to work and to the centre
  matters most. Places far out on a tram line (Hucknall, an hour from the centre) are a poor fit.
- Each listing comes with its times by bus, tram and walking, door to door: to work for 08:45 on a
  Monday, to the centre, to the City Ground and to the Motorpoint Arena. Today work takes 38 minutes
  (bus 68, then the tram). At most 40 minutes to work and 25 to the centre; shorter is better, and
  a quick trip to the grounds is a plus.

## Safety

- Each listing comes with the crime the police recorded within 300 metres over the last year, and its
  level against the other listings in this search, from very low to very high. I do not want to live
  somewhere with a lot of crime (the worst parts of St Ann's are the kind of place I mean): high is a
  real drawback, and very high keeps a place from a 4 or a 5.

## Heating

- Gas central heating is what I want. A heat pump is fine too. Electric heaters or storage heaters are
  a drawback: they cost more to run and are harder to keep warm. The EPC already counts the price of
  the fuel, so electric heating does not make a lower EPC acceptable.

## Other things

- Furnished or unfurnished are both fine; furnished is a plus.
- I have no pets and do not plan any, so a no-pets rule does not matter.
- A garden is not needed; one is a plus.
- I give one month's notice on my house share, so I can move about a month after agreeing a let. A place
  available now or in the next two months suits me; one not available for over three months is a
  drawback.

## Would love

What my ideal home has. None of these is needed, and missing one is never a drawback:

- An EPC A (a B comes close).
- A short, direct trip: one bus or tram with no change, 25 minutes or less to work and 15 or less to the
  centre.
- Crime low or very low.
- A supermarket (Tesco, Lidl, Aldi or similar) a short walk away, when the listing says so.
- A quiet residential street, when the listing says so (not on a main road or above shops or bars).
- Private parking, such as a driveway.
- A garden, or furnished.

## The note on each listing

The page scores each listing out of 10 itself, from the facts: space, EPC, the times to work and the
centre, crime, heating, and the "Would love" things. The note says in a few words what fits me and the
main drawback, naming any "Would love" things it has. A studio, a room in a shared house, students only
or a short let is no home for me; very high crime is a big drawback.
