wants.md above says what the reader wants from a rented home in Nottingham. Then come rental listings from Rightmove and OpenRent (OpenRent's are let by private landlords), each under a ref (h1, h2…) with its rent a month, bedrooms, type and address, what the agent or landlord filled in, the key features, its times by bus, tram and walking to the places the reader goes, the crime recorded around it, its nearest supermarket, its certificate on the EPC register when one was matched, an estimate of what it costs a month all in, its rent against the listings like it up now, a deposit check when the deposit is over the legal cap, and the description. A listing's EPC chart images come right after its text, under the line "h12's EPC chart:"; a listing without one says so.

For every listing:
- `epcBand`, `epcScore` and `epcFrom`: the current energy rating. When the listing has a chart, take it from its own Energy Efficiency Rating chart, the one right after its text: the number in the Current column (not Potential) and its band. Ignore an Environmental Impact (CO2) chart. When there is no chart, or it is not an Energy Efficiency Rating chart, take it from the text: a line giving its certificate on the GOV.UK register or the EPC register, or the agent's or landlord's words ("EPC rating C", "EPC: C", "Energy rating 72"). When neither gives it, `unknown` and 0. Never guess a rating from the age or look of the place.
- `bills`, `billsWhat`, `pets`, `livingRoom`, `garden`, `heating`, `parking`, `street`, `sqm` and `redFlags`: only what the listing says, as their descriptions explain. "not said" when it says nothing.
- `fitWhy`: a note of at most 20 words on how the listing fits the reader in wants.md: what fits and the main drawback, weighing the rent (with what it comes to a month all in, when that is given in full), the bedrooms and living space, the EPC and the heating, the times to work and the centre against wants.md's limits, the crime level, the red flags and the "Would love" things it has. The page scores each listing out of 10 itself, from these facts.

Every listing goes in `homes` exactly once, by its ref.

Deliver the reading by calling the StructuredOutput tool with it as the arguments (a `homes` array, not a string). Don't write it or any JSON as text.
