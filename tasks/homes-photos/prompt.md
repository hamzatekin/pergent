Above are rental listings in Nottingham, each under a ref (p1, p2…) with its photos right after its line. The reader rents alone and wants to know, before going to a viewing, what state each home is in.

For each listing, look at every one of its photos and give:

- `bathroom` and `kitchen`: modern, fine, dated, or not shown, as the photos show them. A bathroom with a recent suite, tiling and shower is modern; an older one that is clean and in good order is fine; an old suite, worn or stained tiling, or one that needs updating is dated. Not shown when no photo shows that room.
- `heating`: radiators when a photo shows water-filled panel radiators (pipes and a valve at the bottom) or a boiler; electric heaters when it shows wall-mounted electric panel heaters or bulky storage heaters with dials and no pipes; not shown when no heater can be made out. Many listings never say how they are heated, so look for it in every room.
- `problems`: only what a tenant would want fixed before moving in that a photo actually shows, a few words each naming the room: black mould around a window or on a ceiling, a damp patch or water stain, peeling or bubbling paint or wallpaper, cracks in a wall or ceiling, broken or missing fittings, badly stained or damaged flooring. At most 4. Old-fashioned decor, furniture, clutter and small marks are not problems. Leave it empty when the photos show none, and leave out anything you are not sure of.
- `note`: the rest of the home's state in the photos in at most 15 words, without repeating the bathroom, kitchen or heating: the decoration, floors, light and space, and which rooms have no photo, like "freshly painted, new carpets, bright lounge; no bedroom photo" or "tired throughout, worn carpets, small dark bedroom".

Give every listing, in the order given. Call StructuredOutput once with the `listings` array as its arguments.
